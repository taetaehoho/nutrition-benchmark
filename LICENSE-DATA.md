## Third-party material

- **Nutrition5k.** The reference labels in `data/` and the photos in `figures/example_inputs*.jpg` come
  from Nutrition5k (Thames, Karpur, Norris, Xia, Panait, Weyand and Sim, "Nutrition5k: Towards Automatic
  Nutritional Understanding of Generic Food", CVPR 2021;
  [dataset](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Nutrition5k)), released under CC BY 4.0. The photos are resized;
  238 dish totals that were published as all zeros are rebuilt from the dataset's own per-ingredient
  values, and 5 labels are flagged as annotation errors. Keep this credit when you reuse them.
- **Smart24Food.** The atlas (Contreras-Guillén et al., PLOS ONE 21(9): e0359331, 2026,
  [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0359331](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359331);
  [smart24food.uap.edu.ar/atlas](https://smart24food.uap.edu.ar/atlas/)) states no licence for its images or nutrient
  values, so this repository contains none of them: only the models' answers and links to the atlas's own
  photos. The CC BY 4.0 grant above covers those answers, not the atlas's content. The photos themselves
  were still sent to the model services for this evaluation: all 2,031 in the main run (some again in the
  unpublished pilots, 20-photo repeat panel and partial rerun), and the 395 spoon and ladle photos again,
  rebuilt on white. That includes OpenAI (through Codex), Anthropic (through Claude Code) and Muse's
  OpenCode Go "Contributor" route, which may train on submitted content; the ChatGPT and Claude accounts'
  data-use settings were not recorded.

  repository holds in them are released under CC BY 4.0, like everything else outside `code/` and
